## Programs

### Beginner (Ages 3-6)
- Introduction to rhythmic gymnastics
- Focuses on: flexibility, strength, coordination, balance
- Basics: body elements, acrobatics (rolls, cartwheels, bridges), dance
- Apparatus: rope, hoop, ball, clubs, ribbon
- No experience necessary

### Intermediate (Ages 7+)
- Recreational level for ages 7 and up
- Same focus areas as beginner with more advanced techniques
- Apparatus: rope, hoop, ball, clubs, ribbon
- No experience necessary

### XCEL Recreational Program
- Fun, engaging intro for all skill levels
- Emphasizes rhythmic movement, coordination, artistic expression
- Age-appropriate levels: Xcel A, B, C, and D
- Opportunity to compete locally during the season
- Supportive and inclusive environment

### Competitive Team (Level 3-10)
- Top-tier training for aspiring elite gymnasts
- Training: 4-5 days/week, 4 hours per session
- Competitions: local, state, regional, national, and international
- Emphasizes: technique, artistry, physical fitness, discipline, teamwork
