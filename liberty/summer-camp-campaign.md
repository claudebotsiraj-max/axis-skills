| Day | Post | Goal |
|-----|------|------|

| Day | Post | Goal |
|-----|------|------|

| Wed | Reel: "Day in the life at Liberty summer camp" (use past footage or B-roll) | Show the experience |

| Day | Post | Goal |
|-----|------|------|

| Wed | **Post C**: FOMO / "Spots Filling Fast" post | Drive immediate action |
| Fri | TikTok: "POV: Dropping your kid off at summer camp at NJ's only Olympic gym" | Viral format, aspirational |

### Week 4: FINAL PUSH & LAST CALL
| Day | Post | Goal |
|-----|------|------|

**Throughout all 4 weeks:** Daily IG stories with camp prep content, Q&A stickers, countdown timers, and parent DM screenshots (with permission).

## Design Notes

- **Primary overlay colors:** Deep navy (#1a1a2e), deep purple (#2d1b4e), burgundy (#4a1942)
- **Urgency overlay:** Dark red (#8b0000) — use sparingly, only for FOMO posts
- **Text color:** Always white (#FFFFFF), bold sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- **Accent elements:** Gold (#D4AF37) for stars, checkmarks, or "Olympic" callouts
- **Brand consistency:** Every post should feel premium, editorial, and confident — not clipart-y or over-designed
- **Photo treatment:** Real Liberty photos with color overlay — never stock images
