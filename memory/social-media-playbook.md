# Social Media Growth Playbook — 2026

## 🎯 Universal Rules (All Platforms)

### The Algorithm Cheat Code
Every algorithm in 2026 optimizes for ONE thing: **keeping users on the platform longer**. Your job is to create content that makes people stop scrolling, engage, and share.

### The 3 Metrics That Matter Most
1. **Watch time / dwell time** — How long someone stays on your content
2. **Shares / sends** — The #1 growth signal on every platform
3. **Saves** — Tells the algorithm "this is worth coming back to"

Likes and comments matter less than shares and saves. Optimize accordingly.

### The Hook Formula (First 1-3 Seconds)
- Open with a bold claim, question, or visual pattern interrupt
- Never start with "Hey guys" or a logo intro
- Show the payoff preview first, then explain
- Text hooks on screen in the first frame

### Posting Cadence
| Platform | Optimal Frequency | Best Times (ET) |
|----------|------------------|-----------------|
| TikTok | 1-3x/day | 7AM, 12PM, 7-9PM |
| Instagram Reels | 4-7x/week | 6-9AM, 12PM, 7-9PM |
| Instagram Feed | 3-5x/week | 8-11AM |
| Instagram Stories | Daily (3-7 frames) | Throughout the day |
| X/Twitter | 2-5x/day | 8-10AM, 12-1PM, 5-6PM |
| Threads | 1-3x/day | Mirror Instagram times |
| LinkedIn | 2-3x/week | 7-8AM, 12PM, 5-6PM |

## 📱 TikTok

### How the Algorithm Works
- **User interactions** (HIGH weight): watch time, likes, shares, comments, "Not Interested"
- **Video information** (MEDIUM): captions, hashtags, sounds, effects
- **User information** (LOW): device, language, country

### Growth Tactics
1. **Find your community** — TikTok is built around subcultures (#FinTok, #CarTok, #TeslaTok). Content that resonates within a niche gets amplified to that audience
2. **Hook in first 1-2 seconds** — Watch time is the #1 signal. If they scroll past in 2 seconds, you're dead
3. **Loop your videos** — End flows into beginning → viewers rewatch without realizing → massive watch time boost
4. **Use trending sounds** — Even at low volume under a voiceover. Algorithm categorizes via audio
5. **Optimal length**: 15-60 seconds for growth, 1-3 min for depth. Under 15s = not enough signal
6. **Post 1-3x daily** — More volume = more shots at the algorithm. One flop doesn't matter
7. **Reply to comments with video** — Creates threads, boosts engagement, signals active creator
8. **Stitch/Duet popular content** — Piggyback on existing reach
9. **High-quality > quantity** — TikTok data: high-quality videos get **40x follower growth** vs low-quality
10. **Cross-post carefully** — Remove watermarks. TikTok deprioritizes content with IG/YT watermarks

### TikTok-Specific Hacks
- Use TikTok Creative Center to find trending sounds/hashtags
- Pin your 3 best-performing videos to your profile
- Go live once you hit 1K followers — live gets priority push
- Engage in the first 30 min after posting (reply to every comment)

## 📸 Instagram

### How the Algorithm Works (2026)
Each section has its OWN algorithm:
- **Feed**: Relationship signals + recency + engagement velocity
- **Reels**: Watch time + rewatches + shares. Entertainment-first lens
- **Stories**: Viewing history + DM closeness + engagement frequency
- **Explore**: Activity patterns + post popularity + content similarity

### The 3 Key Metrics (per Adam Mosseri)
1. **Average watch time**
2. **Likes per reach**
3. **Sends per reach** (shares via DM — the most important one)

### Growth Tactics
1. **Reels are king** — Instagram is pushing Reels hard. They get 2-3x the reach of static posts
2. **Carousels for saves** — Educational carousels get saved more than any other format. Saves = long-term reach
3. **Strong hooks + captions on** — Mosseri explicitly says: hook, captions, audio, no watermarks, under 3 min
4. **Original content only** — IG actively deprioritizes reposted/watermarked content
5. **DM engagement matters** — The algorithm treats DM conversations as a strong relationship signal. Encourage "DM me" CTAs
6. **Collaborate feature** — Co-author posts with other creators. Appears on both profiles' followers feeds
7. **Hashtags: 3-5 targeted > 30 random** — Instagram confirmed fewer, relevant hashtags work better
8. **Stories with interaction stickers** — Polls, questions, sliders. Every tap = engagement signal
9. **Consistent aesthetic isn't required anymore** — Focus on value over grid aesthetics
10. **Check Account Status** — Make sure you're eligible for recommendations (Settings > Account Status)

### Instagram-Specific Hacks
- Share Reels to Stories for double exposure
- Use "Add Yours" stickers on Stories for viral chains
- Respond to EVERY comment in the first hour
- Post carousels as "mini-blogs" — swipe-through tutorials perform insanely well
- Use alt text on images (helps with Explore discovery)

## 🐦 X / Twitter

### How the Algorithm Works
1. **Candidate sourcing**: Picks 1,500 tweets from followed + recommended accounts
2. **Ranking**: ML model scores by likelihood you'll reply, retweet, or like
3. **Filtering**: Removes muted/blocked, balances followed vs recommended
4. **Serving**: Mixes in ads, delivers to For You feed

### Ranking Signals (Weight Order)
- **Replies** (highest weight) — Tweets likely to get replies rank highest
- **Retweets/Reposts** 
- **Likes**
- **Profile visits after viewing**
- **Video watch time**

### Growth Tactics
1. **Post 2-5x daily** — X rewards volume. Threads count as one post
2. **Get verified (X Premium)** — Verified accounts get boosted in For You tab
3. **Quote tweet > retweet** — Adds your take, gets shown to YOUR followers more
4. **Threads perform 2-3x** — Multi-tweet threads get more engagement than single tweets
5. **Engage in replies** — Reply to bigger accounts in your niche. If they engage back, their followers see you
6. **Images/video boost reach ~35%** — Pure text is fine but media gets priority
7. **Ask questions** — Questions drive replies, replies are the highest-weighted engagement
8. **Timing matters more on X** — Half-life of a tweet is ~18 minutes. Post when your audience is online
9. **Use Lists to engage** — Monitor your niche's top voices, engage with their posts quickly
10. **Pin your best tweet** — First thing profile visitors see

### X-Specific Hacks
- Long-form posts (Notes) get extra algorithmic push
- Spaces (audio) appearances boost your profile visibility
- Don't include external links in the tweet body — algorithm deprioritizes them. Put links in replies instead
- Edit tweets to fix typos (doesn't kill reach anymore)

## 🔗 LinkedIn

### Growth Tactics
1. **Document posts (carousels)** — Highest engagement format on LinkedIn
2. **Personal stories > corporate** — Vulnerability and lessons learned outperform announcements
3. **Dwell time is key** — Longer posts that keep people reading get pushed
4. **Comment on posts from larger accounts** — Your comment is seen by THEIR network
5. **Post early morning** — LinkedIn peaks 7-8 AM when people check before work

## 🔥 Cross-Platform Strategy

### Content Repurposing Waterfall
1. **Create one hero piece** (TikTok video or IG Reel)
2. **Cross-post to**: Reels → TikTok → YouTube Shorts → Threads (text summary) → X (thread) → LinkedIn (lesson)
3. **Remove watermarks** between platforms (use SnapTik or download original)
4. **Adjust captions** for each platform's tone (TikTok casual, LinkedIn professional, X punchy)

### Growth Multipliers
- **Engagement pods** — Small groups (5-10 creators) who engage with each other's content in the first 30 min
- **Comment sections** — Leave genuinely valuable comments on viral posts in your niche. Profile clicks from comments = free followers
- **Collaborations** — Cross-promote with creators at similar follower counts. Both audiences benefit
- **SEO on social** — People search TikTok/IG like Google now. Use keywords in captions, not just hashtags
- **Consistency > virality** — One viral post means nothing without consistent follow-up content

### What to AVOID
- ❌ Buying followers (tanks your engagement rate, algorithms detect it)
- ❌ Follow/unfollow tactics (platforms penalize this)
- ❌ Posting the same exact content everywhere without adapting
- ❌ Ignoring comments (algorithm reads creator responsiveness)
- ❌ Posting at random times (use analytics to find YOUR audience's peak)
- ❌ Watermarked cross-posts (especially TikTok logo on IG)
- ❌ Engagement bait ("Like if you agree!") — platforms are cracking down
